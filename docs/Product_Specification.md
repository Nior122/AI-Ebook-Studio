# Product Specification

## Vision

AI Ebook Studio will help authors and publishers plan, produce, validate, and package ebooks with AI-assisted workflows while keeping users in control of quality, brand, and compliance.

## Stage 1 Scope

This stage creates only the project foundation. Product workflows are documented, but not implemented.

## Future Product Areas

- Project workspace for books and editions
- Manuscript planning and writing assistance
- Editing and revision workflows
- Cover and interior image generation
- KDP-oriented validation
- Export to publishing-ready formats
- Marketing copy generation
- Translation workflows

## Non-Goals For Stage 1

- No authentication screens or backend auth logic
- No AI calls
- No image generation
- No export engine
- No marketplace publishing automation
